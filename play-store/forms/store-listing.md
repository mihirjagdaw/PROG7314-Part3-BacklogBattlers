## Full description

```
Backlog Battlers turns the games you never finished into a monthly competition with your friends.

Search for a game and add it to your library. You can also link Steam to import the games you own with your hours played. Every achievement Steam confirms earns you points, and rarer ones are worth more. Finishing a game earns completion points based on how long it usually takes. Beat the average and you get a bonus.

Add your friends and see where you stand on the monthly leaderboard. Points reset on the first of each month. The activity feed shows what your friends have finished and unlocked. Push notifications tell you when a friend sends a request or passes you in the standings. You can switch off each type in Settings.

Your profile shows your level and the badges you have earned.

The app keeps working when your signal drops. Changes to your library are saved on your phone and sync once you are back online.

You sign in with Google, and you can lock the app with your fingerprint. The app is also translated into Afrikaans and isiZulu.

You can delete your account and everything linked to it from Settings.

Game information comes from IGDB. Achievement data comes from the Steam Web API. Backlog Battlers is not affiliated with or endorsed by Steam or IGDB.
```
