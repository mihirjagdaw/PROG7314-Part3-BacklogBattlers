# Backlog Battlers Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026.

Backlog Battlers is an Android app built by Dylan Jurgens, Andre Fourie and Mihir Jagdaw, students on the PROG7314 module. This page explains what the app stores about you and how to delete it.

## What we store

**From your Google account.** When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, profile picture link and Google account ID. Your name becomes your display name, which you can change. Your email identifies your account and is never shown to other users.

**What you do in the app.** Your game library, hours played, unlocked achievements, finished games, points, friends and friend requests, activity feed, settings and the profile picture you pick from the app's own set.

**Steam, if you link it.** Your Steam ID. We use it to read your owned games with their hours played, and your unlocked achievements, from Steam's public Web API. This only works when your Steam game details are public. You can unlink Steam in Settings > Connected Accounts.

**Your phone's notification token.** Firebase Cloud Messaging gives the app a token so our server can send you push notifications. You can turn off each notification type in Settings.

**Request logs.** Our server logs each request with the method, path, result, time taken and your internal account number. These logs never contain your name, email, sign in tokens, search text or anything you typed.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, messages or advertising ID. The app has no ads and no analytics.

Fingerprint unlock is handled by Android on your phone. The app is only told whether the check passed. It never receives your fingerprint.

## How we use it

Only to run the app. That means signing you in, showing your library, working out your points, ranking you against your friends, filling the activity feed and sending the notifications you have turned on. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

## Who can see it

Other users can find you by display name when they search for friends. They see your display name and profile picture. Once you are friends they can also see your points, level, badges, achievement showcase and recent activity. You can hide your badges and showcase from your profile. No other user can see your email address.

## Where it is kept

The API and its PostgreSQL database run on Microsoft Azure in the UAE North region. Push notifications are delivered through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. Both act only as service providers for us.

On your phone, the app keeps a copy of your library so it works offline. Your sign in tokens are encrypted with a key held in the phone's Android Keystore. They are left out of Android backups.

All traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS.

## How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Request logs are kept by the hosting service for troubleshooting.

## Delete your account

In the app, open **Settings**, tap **Delete Account** and confirm. This removes your account and everything linked to it straight away. That includes your profile, library, achievements, points, friends, settings, notification tokens, Steam link and activity feed entries. It cannot be undone.

## Children

Backlog Battlers is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.
